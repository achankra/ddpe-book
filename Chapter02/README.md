# Chapter 2: Understanding Domains in an Engineering Context

This chapter explores how to identify, map, and prioritize business domains within an engineering organization — the essential first step before applying Domain-Driven Design to platform engineering.

## Files

### `Domain Identification Scorecard.xlsx`
Score each domain in your organization against business criticality, engineering complexity, regulatory burden, and platform readiness. The output is a prioritized domain map for platform onboarding decisions.

**How to use:** Open in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, or LibreOffice Calc. For each domain in your organization, fill in the scoring criteria across each assessment dimension. The workbook calculates a composite score and produces a prioritized domain map that helps platform teams decide which domains to onboard first and where domain-specific platform capabilities will deliver the most value.

> **Interactive version available:** An interactive version of this assessment is available at [ddpe.platformetrics.com](https://ddpe.platformetrics.com).

## Key Concepts

This chapter covers organizational progression of domain thinking, layered domain architecture, Conway's Law in platform design, and developer-centric domain mapping. The scorecard translates these concepts into a practical assessment tool.
